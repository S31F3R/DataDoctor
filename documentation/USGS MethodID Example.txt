DataID format: Site-Method-Parameter

  Modern (time_series_id — Aquarius-style 32-char hex UID):
    09428500-14eef6ee402b4c50a7341a0efccf0cd4-00065
    → api.waterdata.usgs.gov OGC continuous/daily

  Legacy (numeric methodID):
    09428500-158041-00065
    → waterservices.usgs.gov nwis/iv or nwis/dv

Routing is automatic from the method segment.
